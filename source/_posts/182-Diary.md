This evening, my swimming tutor sent me a message and asked me to go swimming. So I went to Chen Jinglun Sports College with my swimming fins and [<abbr title="an excellent training device for developing endurance and upper body strength">pull bouy</abbr>](http://holdurhand.com/archives/165.html) and wanted to train myself.

My tutor herself could not swim today for some reasons^-^, so she stood by the pool, giving us some instructions now and then, which I appreciate very much.

After I swam for a while, the tutor asked me to have a competition with Starriver, who is senior to me. Actually, today is the eighth day I went to swim in a row. I myself was eager to know what the effect of training everyday, too. So I said yes.

I have tried my best and beat Starriver at the 50-meter freestyle but failed at the 25-meter breaststroke. My tutor said that my movements of both style were not good enough, I won the game just by my physical strength.

So, today, I have decided to train harder in the days to come to improve my movements of these two swimming styles.
