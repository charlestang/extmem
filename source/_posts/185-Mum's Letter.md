Hi, my boy,

I’m so delighted that you <em>are off to a start</em> \[have started\]to <em>have a go</em> \[have a try\]at blogging in English. This is really a good thing. You’re sure to make progress in writing if you <em>keep up</em> but not just <em>follow the trend</em> and do a lot of reading in English, as you know, the improvement of your writing ability <em>depends largely on</em> your English input. I’d like very much to be your pen pal as well as your teacher if you like. I do not mean that my English is much better than yours, but that I also want to have a go at improving my English writing. I do hope that <em>I’m of some help</em> to your English study, by, for example, <em>making some revision of</em> (做一些修改) your passage <em>for your reference only</em> (仅供你参考). <em>For the sake of</em> （为了）saving (免去/省去)your time in looking up the dictionary, I’ll underline the phrases or idioms or give an English or Chinese explanation in brackets or parentheses (括号), as is done above (如上文所示) if you don’t mind my doing so.

Lovingly

Mum
